# Kanyi Website 2.0 — Content Review Register

This register separates content already present in the supplied website from new copy that requires confirmation by Kanyi J. & Company Advocates.

## Preserved from the supplied website

- The nine practice-area titles and their existing short descriptions.
- The three office labels, addresses, telephone strings, email strings and map search destinations.
- The firm contact email and primary telephone/WhatsApp display numbers.
- Existing attorney profile text and portraits (not rewritten in this implementation).

## Requires firm confirmation before final release

- Whether the current office addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and map pins remain correct.
- Whether the current practice-area descriptions remain approved and sufficiently current.
- Whether the consultation-request fields and response expectations match the firm's intake process.
- Whether the website FAQ wording accurately reflects the firm's preferred client-intake and confidentiality process.
- Any future Legal Insights articles, legal explanations, case studies, testimonials, attorney biographies or claims of expertise.
- Official LinkedIn, Facebook and other social profile URLs before adding social links back to the footer.

## Editorial safeguards

- Do not publish legal advice generated by the website or an AI system.
- Articles and legal FAQs require review and approval by an authorised advocate before publication.
- Do not invite confidential case files, identity documents or sensitive personal information through public forms or WhatsApp.
- A website enquiry is not a confirmed appointment and does not itself establish a lawyer-client relationship.
